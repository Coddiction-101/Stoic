This is a living, self-built compendium — a structured archive of everything worth understanding, questioning, and researching. It covers physics, consciousness, psychology, philosophy, power, technology, music, mental health, mysteries, and the deeply unsettling corners of human existence.

It is not a textbook. It is a map of curiosity.

## How to Use This

**If you want to explore an idea** — search for the keyword inside `compendium.md`. Every section is clearly headed and subsectioned.

**If you want to add a topic** — find the closest section by theme and append it. No topic is too small or too weird.

**If you want to go deep on something** — use the section as a keyword list. Each entry is a research rabbit hole, not a definition.

## Philosophy Behind This

Most knowledge lives in silos — science here, philosophy there, mental health somewhere else. This compendium tears down those walls. A question like *"Why do I feel empty?"* lives simultaneously in neuroscience, existentialism, cultural conditioning, digital addiction, and ancient philosophy. This repo treats it that way.

> *The goal is not to have answers. The goal is to ask better questions.*
