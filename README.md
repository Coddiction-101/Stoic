# **Useful Life Hacks Learned from Others**

- **Adopt the 1% Mindset**: Focus on small, incremental improvements.
- **Implement the 90-90-1 Rule**: Spend the first 90 minutes of your day on your most important task for 90 days.
- **8+8+8 Rule**: Balance your day with 8 hours of work, 8 hours of leisure, and 8 hours of sleep.
- **The 3/2/1 Rule for Better Sleep**: 3 hours before bed, no food; 2 hours, no work; 1 hour, no screens.
- **The Rule of 100**: Focus on 100% of your effort on one task at a time.
- **50/30/20 Budget Rule**: Allocate 50% of income to needs, 30% to wants, and 20% to savings.
- **Practice the 10/10/10 Rule**: Consider the impact of your decisions in 10 minutes, 10 months, and 10 years.
- **Implement the 5 Second Rule (Mel Robbins)**: Take action within 5 seconds to overcome hesitation.
- **3-3-3 Method**: When feeling anxious, name 3 things you see, 3 sounds you hear, and 3 things you can touch.
- **Build a ‘To Don’t’ Rule**: List things you will not do to maintain focus.
- **Embrace Mono-Tasking (Deep Work)**: Focus on one task at a time for better productivity.
- **Leverage the Zeigarnik Effect**: Use unfinished tasks to motivate you to complete them.
- **The Everyday Razor**: Simplify decisions by focusing on what truly matters.
- **30 for 30 Challenge**: Dedicate 30 minutes a day for 30 days to a new skill or habit.
